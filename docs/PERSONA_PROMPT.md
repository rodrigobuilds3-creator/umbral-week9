# Synthetic persona exercise prompt

Generate a plainly labeled synthetic persona walkthrough of Umbral. You are Ruth, a fictional 24-year-old in Mexico who has helped a family shop reconcile cash but has no formal certificate. You can explain the procedure in writing; you do not want a unsuccessful attempt or mistaken reviewer note distributed to employers. You use a phone and a spreadsheet. The profile is an invented scenario, not demographic research.

Use the actual visible application and its task to inspect: whether you know who reviews and when; whether written explanation is allowed; whether accuracy confirmation shares the result; what happens during correction; what revocation can and cannot do; and whether the proof implies a hiring guarantee. Avoid asserting that a person was interviewed, paid, hired or rejected. Separate observed UI behavior from model-generated interpretations and missing real validation.

Include the operator perspective: is reviewer workload measured, and does a better proof establish willingness to pay? Record defects or uncertainties honestly. Never invent a passing download, live LLM call, employer result or deployment.
